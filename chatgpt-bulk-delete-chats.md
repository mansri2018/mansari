# Privacy Policy — Free ChatGPT Bulk Delete Chats

**Last updated:** 16 November 2026

Free ChatGPT Bulk Delete Chats is provided by Mansari. This policy explains how the extension handles information when you use its chat-cleanup and optional paid features.

## Information the extension handles

**ChatGPT sidebar information:** The extension accesses conversation links and identifiers to display selection controls and perform the operations you request. It does not read or collect the message bodies inside your conversations.

**ChatGPT authentication:** The extension temporarily accesses your existing ChatGPT session token to authorise requests to ChatGPT. It does not ask for your ChatGPT password or send your ChatGPT session token to ExtensionPay, Stripe, or the publisher.

**Usage and operation state:** The extension stores daily free-delete usage, conversation identifiers associated with pending or recent operations, batch progress, and recovery information in browser extension storage.

**Purchase and access information:** ExtensionPay handles Pro access verification and purchase restoration. Information may include an email address, an access identifier, purchase status, and purchase-related dates. Its SDK may use browser-synchronised storage where available.

**Payments:** Payments take place through ExtensionPay and Stripe. They process the information needed for checkout, payment processing, fraud prevention, and account administration. The extension does not receive or store full payment-card numbers.

## How information is used

Information is used to:

- Display chat-selection controls and carry out requested operations.
- Apply the free daily allowance.
- Display progress and recover already-started operations.
- Verify and restore Pro access.
- Respond to support requests.

For Pro operations, selected chats are archived before permanent deletion is attempted. Already-started operations may be recovered when ChatGPT is opened again. Closing the browser does not guarantee that permanent deletion has finished.

## Sharing and service providers

ChatGPT requests, including the authentication and conversation identifiers required for those requests, are sent to ChatGPT/OpenAI.

Purchase and access information is handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe. The extension does not send your conversation message bodies or ChatGPT session token to these payment providers.

If you contact support, the publisher receives the information you choose to provide.

**Relevant provider policies:**

- [OpenAI](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/)
- [ExtensionPay](https://extensionpay.com/privacy)
- [Stripe](https://stripe.com/privacy)

These providers may independently process technical information, such as IP addresses, under their own policies.

## Storage and retention

Operational information is stored in browser extension storage. Pending-operation information is retained as needed for recovery. Completed batch history becomes eligible for cleanup after seven days; cleanup takes place during subsequent state updates rather than through a guaranteed daily deletion schedule.

Purchase-access information may remain in local or browser-synchronised storage until cleared. ExtensionPay and Stripe retain their records under their own policies and applicable requirements.

Clearing extension storage removes local operational information and may prevent recovery of unfinished operations. It does not remove records held by payment providers.

## Your choices

You can use the free features without purchasing Pro. You can stop remaining operations, disable or uninstall the extension, and clear its stored data. An already-sent request may still finish after you press Stop. Permanent chat deletion cannot be undone.

For privacy questions or requests concerning information held by the publisher, contact mnsri101986@gmail.com. Requests concerning provider-held information may also require contacting the relevant provider.

## Security and limited use

The extension uses HTTPS for network requests. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

The extension does not include advertising or behavioural analytics. User data is not sold, used for unrelated advertising, or used to determine creditworthiness or eligibility for lending.

The use of information by this extension complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

## Changes

This policy will be updated if the extension’s data practices change. Changes requiring additional disclosure or consent will be presented as required.
