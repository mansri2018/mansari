PRIVACY POLICY — CHATGPT AUDIO DOWNLOADER Last updated: November 15, 2026 This policy explains how ChatGPT Audio Downloader handles information when you use the extension. INFORMATION THE EXTENSION PROCESSES The extension operates on chatgpt.com. To provide audio downloads and voice selection, it processes: • Generated audio and the request information needed to identify and retrieve that audio, including conversation and message identifiers. • Your selected voice. • Your toolbar position. • Information returned by ChatGPT’s signed-in session endpoint, including an access token used temporarily to save your voice preference. The extension does not save your access token to persistent storage or intentionally log it. HOW INFORMATION IS USED Information is used to play and download generated audio, save voice preferences, avoid duplicate audio requests, and remember the toolbar’s position. STORAGE AND RETENTION Audio and playback information are held temporarily in the page’s memory. They are discarded when the page is unloaded, such as when the tab is closed or refreshed. The toolbar position is saved in local storage associated with chatgpt.com in your browser. It remains until that stored setting or the site’s local storage is cleared. This storage is accessible to scripts running on that site. Your voice preference is saved to your ChatGPT account through ChatGPT’s existing service. Downloaded audio files remain on your device until you delete them. NETWORK REQUESTS AND THIRD PARTIES The extension communicates with ChatGPT’s existing services to retrieve audio and save voice preferences. Those services receive the information needed to fulfil these requests and operate under their own privacy policies. The extension does not send this information to developer-operated servers, advertising services, or analytics providers. The developer does not sell personal information. SITE ACCESS The extension runs on chatgpt.com to add its controls and interact with audio playback and voice settings. YOUR CHOICES You can stop using the extension or uninstall it at any time. Uninstalling does not automatically delete downloaded audio, clear chatgpt.com local storage, or restore a previous ChatGPT voice preference. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY This policy may be updated when the extension’s functionality or data practices change. The updated policy will include a revised date.
